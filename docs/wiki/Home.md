# Lands of Lore VR

*Lands of Lore: The Throne of Chaos* (Westwood, 1993) in virtual reality on **Meta Quest 3**, standalone, no PC needed.
The original game runs on [ScummVM](https://www.scummvm.org); the app adds two ways to play:

- **Immersive mode**: you walk inside the dungeon, rebuilt in 3D around you from your own game files. The game screen comes up only when needed (shops, conversations, the map, menus).
- **Classic view**: the original game on a big screen, in a medieval library, in your own room (mixed reality) or on an old 486 PC, with a movable interface.

At first launch, a window asks which one you want; you can switch at any time from "VR controls" in the game menu (the floppy on your left wrist).

**The game files are not included**: you need your own copy of the game (CD or GOG version).
