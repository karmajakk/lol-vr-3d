# Immersive mode

**English** · [Français](fr/Immersive-mode.md) · [Deutsch](de/Immersive-mode.md)

The dungeon is rebuilt in true 3D around you, at real size, from your own game files: walls, decorations, doors, floors and ceilings, monsters and items (drawn by the game itself and always facing you). There is only one view: no mixed reality and no 486 PC in this mode.

- **Walking**: the left stick points relative to where you look. Forward walks where you look, sideways is a side step. When you stand still, the party quietly turns to face where you look (not while you aim at a window), so the wall "in front" and attacks are the ones you see.
- **Turning**: the right stick turns with a very short fade, to stay comfortable.
- **Clicking in the dungeon**: aim at a wall around the party (door buttons, levers, keyholes, bushes, nests, holes in trees) or at the floor in front of you (items) and pull the trigger.
- **Shops and other regions**: their doors and pictures are walls; walk into them and the game screen shows the shop, or the new region appears. In shops and conversations, the message line with the choices is joined under the game screen; when the game opens its large text box (conversations, with the speaker's portrait in it, when the game's text is on), the whole box is joined there.
- **Items**: take one from the inventory and aim ahead into the dungeon to throw it or drop it; the item stays on your pointer.
- **The game screen** appears by itself for shops, conversations, scenes, the map, menus and character sheets, and hides again when they end.
- **Light**: dark places stay dark until you light the lamp, following the game's own brightness.
- **Spells**: their effects, as the game draws them over its view, appear in the dungeon in front of the party while the spell is cast.

Not there yet: doors that slide open (they jump from closed to open), thrown objects in flight.

Switch to the classic view in "VR controls" (game menu, floppy on the left wrist): "VR mode", Classic or Mixed reality.
