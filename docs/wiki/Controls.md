# Controls

| Controller | Action |
|---|---|
| Aim + trigger | Left click (the hand that clicks becomes the pointer) |
| Aim + grip | Right click |
| Left stick | Walk forward, back, strafe (one direction at a time). In immersive mode, relative to where you look |
| Right stick (left/right) | Turn |
| Right stick (up/down) | In the dungeon: next / previous character |
| A | In the dungeon: **attack** with the selected character. Elsewhere: Enter |
| B | In the dungeon: open the selected character's **magic**. Elsewhere: Escape (skip a cutscene) |
| Y | Map |
| **X** | **Right click** where you aim: drink a potion, use an item (same as the grip) |
| Menu button (left controller) | ScummVM menu: save, load, quit |
| **Floppy on the left wrist** | The game menu (load, save...), and in it "VR controls": language, immersive mode, game screen, controller mapping |
| Right stick click | Recenter the screen, the room and the windows in front of you |
| Short left stick click | Classic view: change scenery (library → mixed reality with frame → mixed reality with CRT monitor) |
| Left stick click held + right stick | Up/down: screen size; left/right: distance |

## Floppy on the wrist, VR controls

Look at your left hand: a small floppy disk appears above it, like the game's options button (which is no longer in the party window). At first launch, a note under your gaze and a light buzz in the left controller point you to it, until you click it once. Click it with the right hand's trigger: the game's own menu opens (load, save, delete a game, game and audio controls, exit). Click the floppy again to close it.

At the bottom of that menu, left of "Resume game", **VR controls** opens the headset's settings in front of you, laid out like the game's options (setting on the left, its value on a button):

- **Language**: switches between English and French.
- **Immersive mode**: on, you walk in the dungeon rebuilt in 3D (see [Immersive mode](Immersive-mode.md)); off, the classic view, with the game on a big screen.
- In immersive mode, **Game screen**: "if needed" (it comes up for shops, conversations, the map, menus) or "always".
- In the classic view, **Classic screen**: on, the whole game image stays on the big screen, like the original game; off, the interface windows float on their own.
- **Controller mapping**: shows a panel summing up every button. Click the panel to close it.
- **Close**.

The keyboard opens by itself when the game asks for a save description, and closes when the save is done.

## Button combat

In the dungeon, one character is "selected": they are framed in yellow in the party column. **A** makes them attack, **B** opens their spell list (then aim at the spell). Flick the right stick up or down to pick the next or previous character. In menus, cutscenes, the map and a character sheet, A and B go back to Enter and Escape; the Escape key is also on the keyboard.

To **drink a potion** or use an item: aim at it in the inventory and press **X** (a right click, like in the original game). The grip of the aiming hand does the same.

## Moving the screen and windows

The screen and the keyboard carry a **brass bar** above them; the game windows carry it on their side, so it never gets in the pointer's way.

1. Aim at the bar (the yellow beam gets longer as soon as you leave the screen and the windows).
2. Squeeze the trigger and keep it squeezed: the object follows your hand.
3. While you hold it, the right stick pushes or pulls it (up/down) and shrinks or enlarges it (left/right).
4. Release to drop it.

Positions are remembered between sessions.

## Resizing

Aim at a corner of the screen or a window, just outside (on the frame, for the screen): a brass bracket appears. Squeeze the trigger, then move your hand away to enlarge or closer to shrink. The window resizes around its center.

## Keyboard

The virtual keyboard opens by itself to name a save. Its top row holds Panels (detached interface or whole image; pushed in, its name in blue, when on), Reset layout and Close.

## Haptics

The controllers vibrate when the party hits or gets hurt, when doors open, on levers and spells.
