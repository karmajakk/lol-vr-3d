## Floppy on the wrist, VR controls

Turn your left hand to look at its back, as you would read a watch: a small floppy disk appears on it (not when the hand just rests in your lap), like the game's options button (which is no longer in the party window). At first launch, a note under your gaze and a light buzz in the left controller point you to it, until you click it once. Click it with the right hand's trigger: the game's own menu opens (load, save, delete a game, game and audio controls, exit). Click the floppy again to close it.

At the bottom of that menu, left of "Resume game", **VR controls** opens the headset's settings in front of you, laid out like the game's options (setting on the left, its value on a button):

- **VR mode**: **Immersive** (you walk in the dungeon rebuilt in 3D, see [Immersive mode](Immersive-mode.md)), **Classic** (the game in a medieval library) or **Mixed reality** (the game in your real room). The first time you pick Classic or Mixed reality, a note reminds you that L3 changes the view.
- In Classic and Mixed reality, **View (L3)**: **Big screen** (the whole game image, like the original game), **Windows** (the interface windows float on their own) or **486 PC** (the game on a curved 90s monitor standing on a 486 PC). A short click of the left stick does the same.
- **Controller mapping**: a panel summing up every button for the profile in use. **Customize** opens the button editor:
  - **Profile** `<` `>`: choose a profile (it is used at once). **Classic**, the default, is always there and cannot be changed or deleted.
  - A, B, X, Y, the grip and the two stick clicks: click an action to switch to the next one (the left arrow goes back): attack, magic, right click, map, recenter, view, Enter, Escape, atlas window, next character, keyboard or nothing. The buttons work that way right away.
  - **Save** (your own profiles), **Save as new** (type a name on the keyboard, Enter saves), **Delete**, **Back**. Changes not saved are dropped when the panel closes.
  - The trigger, the sticks (walk, turn), the left grip (next character), the menu button and the floppy always stay the same.
- **Credits & Requirements**: a short note on what the mod runs on and who made it possible (also on the first-launch window).
- **Close**.

At the bottom: "Made possible by ScummVM & KForestland" ([lands-of-lore-1-re](https://github.com/KForestland/lands-of-lore-1-re)).

The app speaks the language chosen in the game's own options ("Language is" in Game controls): English, French or German.

The keyboard opens by itself when the game asks for a save description, and closes when the save is done.

## Button combat

In the dungeon, one character is "selected": they are framed in yellow in the party column. **A** makes them attack, **B** opens the levels of the spell chosen in the spellbook, level 1 framed; the **left stick click** (immersive mode; right stick click in the other modes) frames the next level the character can cast (1, 2, 3, 4, back to 1), and **B** again casts it. Clicking a level with the trigger works too. Flick the right stick down or up to pick the next or previous character (in the order of the party window). In menus, cutscenes, the map and a character sheet, A and B go back to Enter and Escape; the Escape key is also on the keyboard.

To **drink a potion** or use an item: aim at it in the inventory and press **X** (a right click, like in the original game). The grip of the aiming hand does the same.

## Moving the screen and windows

1. Aim anywhere on the screen or a window.
2. **Hold the trigger** a moment (less than half a second): the object follows your hand. A short press stays an ordinary click, sent when you release.
3. While you hold it, the right stick pushes or pulls it (up/down) and shrinks or enlarges it (left/right).
4. Release to drop it.

Positions are remembered between sessions.

## Resizing

Aim at a corner of the screen or a window, just outside (on the frame, for the screen): a brass bracket appears. Squeeze the trigger, then move your hand away to enlarge or closer to shrink. The window resizes around its center.

## Keyboard

The virtual keyboard opens by itself to name a save. Its top row holds Panels (detached interface or whole image; pushed in, its name in blue, when on), Reset layout and Close.

Type with your fingers: touch a key with the tip of either index finger, as on a real keyboard (a short vibration confirms it); pull the finger back a little to type the next one. Aiming and squeezing the trigger works too.

## Haptics

The controllers vibrate when the party hits or gets hurt, when doors open, on levers and spells.
