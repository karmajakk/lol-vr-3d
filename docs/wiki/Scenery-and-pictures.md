# Scenery and pictures

Besides the immersive mode (the 3D dungeon only), "VR controls" offers two places:

- **Classic**: a medieval library, a closed room with a fireplace, a window, shelves, paintings, banners and relics on pedestals. The view through the window, the light, a painting, the banners and the ambience change with the game area.
- **Mixed reality**: your real room.

In both, three views, switched with a short click of the left stick (L3): the **big screen** (the game in its golden frame), the **windows** (the interface spread around the game view) and the **486 PC** (the game on a curved 90s screen standing on a 486 PC).

Everything is generated by code: no image or sound file ships with the app.

## Your pictures in the library (optional)

Copy PNG or JPEG pictures into the folder:

`Android/data/com.karmajakk.lolvr3d/files/decor`

The **name** tells where each picture goes:

| Name containing | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| `fenetre` (or `castle`) | the view through the window |
| `portrait` (or `king`) | the painting above the fireplace |
| `tableau1` | left wall, in place of the map |
| `tableau2` | back wall, left of the door |
| `tableau3` | left wall, in place of the landscape |
| `tableau4` | back wall, right of the door (frame added only if the picture exists) |

Six pictures at most. A picture with any other name takes the first free spot. The app picks them up a few seconds after you copy them, no restart needed.

These pictures are not provided: like the game files, everyone brings their own.

## Sound

The game sound comes from the screen: it stays in place when you turn your head. There is no ambient sound.
