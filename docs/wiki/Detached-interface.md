# Windows

**English** · [Français](fr/Detached-interface.md) · [Deutsch](de/Detached-interface.md)

While exploring, the game interface is spread over windows you place wherever you like (always in immersive mode; in Classic and Mixed reality with the "Windows" view):

- on the left, the spell scroll, with the message line joined under it; always open;
- on the right, the characters one under the other, the atlas column (lamp, purse) to their right, and the rest button under the purse, all in one window (the options button, the floppy, is on your left wrist);
- below, the inventory;
- further right, once the party has the map, the **Atlas**: the game's own atlas page (same parchment, symbols, font and legend), updated at every step as you explore. It is closed by default: the atlas icon of the game (open book, right column) or the map button opens it, and the "Close" box on the page closes it. You can move it like the other windows.

At first they sit around the screen: the pointer moves from one to the other with no offset, so you can pick up an item on the floor and drop it into the inventory. If you aim just beside a window, the pointer snaps to the nearest one.

Each window is **independent**: moving or resizing the screen doesn't move the others, and each one is moved and resized (corners) on its own: **hold the trigger** anywhere on a window (or on the screen) for a moment and it follows your finger; a short press is an ordinary click. In the library, they don't go through the floor or the walls (but they can still go through shelves and other objects).

During cutscenes, menus, the map and full-width scenes, the whole image comes back by itself. The ScummVM menu is shown as a whole image too.

The **inventory** is a quick bar of 8 slots (3D dungeon); the button on its right unfolds the 40 other slots, and the second button sorts them by kind. Click an item to take it, click a slot to put it down.

A **character sheet** is shown in full on the screen, which widens to the right while it's open. The windows stay where they are; the atlas column goes dark, because the sheet uses that part of the image.

The **Reset layout** button of the keyboard arranges the windows around the screen, wherever it is. To move or resize a window, see [Controls](Controls.md).
