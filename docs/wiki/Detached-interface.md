# Windows

**English** · [Français](fr/Detached-interface.md) · [Deutsch](de/Detached-interface.md)

While exploring, the game interface is spread over windows you place wherever you like (always in immersive mode; in Classic and Mixed reality with the "Windows" view):

- one big block below the view, in a single frame: on the left the spellbook, in the middle the inventory (two quick rows) with the message line and the characters side by side under it, on the right the compass column (compass, atlas, lamp, purse). The block moves as one piece (a single handle, underneath) and grows or shrinks by the corners of its frame;
- above the inventory, two book tabs as in Lands of Lore 2: on the right, two opposed arrows open the whole inventory upwards (six rows), and close it again (the lit arrow shows which way it goes); on the left, three bars sort it (rows 3 to 6; the two quick rows are never sorted). Sorting works with the inventory closed too;
- two buttons on the side of your wrists (thumb side), always there: on the left the floppy (game menu, VR controls), on the right the rest button (ZZZ). Look at the side of your wrist, like a watch, and click it with the other hand;
- on the right, once the party has the map, the **Atlas**: the game's own atlas page (same parchment, symbols, font and legend), updated at every step as you explore. It is closed by default: the atlas icon of the game (open book, in the compass column) or the map button opens it, and the "Close" box on the page closes it. Its arrows open the game's own atlas on the screen, one page turned, to browse the maps of the other levels. You can move it like the other windows.

At first they sit around the screen: the pointer moves from one to the other with no offset, so you can pick up an item on the floor and drop it into the inventory. If you aim just beside a window, the pointer snaps to the nearest one.

Each window is **independent**: moving or resizing the screen doesn't move the others, and each one is moved and resized (corners) on its own: **hold the trigger** anywhere on a window (or on the screen) for a moment and it follows your finger; a short press is an ordinary click. In the library, they don't go through the floor or the walls (but they can still go through shelves and other objects).

During cutscenes, menus, the map and full-width scenes, the whole image comes back by itself. The ScummVM menu is shown as a whole image too.

The **inventory** is a quick bar of 8 slots (3D dungeon); the button on its right unfolds the 40 other slots, and the second button sorts them by kind. Click an item to take it, click a slot to put it down.

A **character sheet** is shown in full on the screen, which widens to the right while it's open. The windows stay where they are; the atlas column goes dark, because the sheet uses that part of the image.

The **Reset layout** button of the keyboard arranges the windows around the screen, wherever it is. To move or resize a window, see [Controls](Controls.md).

## Grouped or separate windows

In immersive mode, "VR controls" → "Interface" cycles through three choices: **Grouped** (the big block described above, its corners in the four corners of its frame), **Windows** (the same windows apart — spellbook, characters with the message line above them, inventory with its tabs, compass column — each with its own handle, placed and resized on its own; the inventory opens upwards) and **Classic** (the game's own bottom bar). In the Classic and Mixed reality VR modes, the "Windows" view is always grouped.

The windows have a thin dark edge, like the lines around the game's own interface areas.
